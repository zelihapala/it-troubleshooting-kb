# How to recognize a phishing email

## Symptom
The user received an email that says their account will be closed, pushes them to act quickly, or contains a suspicious link or attachment.

## Possible causes
An attacker is trying to steal the user's password or personal information.

## Resolution
1. Do not click the link and do not open the attachment.
2. Check the sender address carefully. It may look like the company address but have one letter different.
3. Hover over the link without clicking. If the real address shown at the bottom does not match the text, it is fake.
4. Look for these signs: urgency ("click now"), generic greeting ("Dear customer"), spelling mistakes, unexpected attachments.
5. Forward the email to the security team, then delete it and empty the deleted items folder.

## Verification
The email was reported and deleted, and the user did not click anything.

## Escalation
If the user clicked the link or entered a password, follow the article "Clicked a phishing link" and tell the security team immediately.
