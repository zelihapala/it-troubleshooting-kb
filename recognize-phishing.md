## Summary
This article shows the warning signs of a phishing email and what to do when you receive one.

## Symptoms
- An email says an account will be closed or asks the user to act immediately
- An email contains an unexpected link or attachment

## Cause
An attacker is trying to steal the user's password or personal information.

## Resolution
1. Do not click the link and do not open the attachment.
2. Check the sender address carefully. It may look like the company address but have one letter different.
3. Hover over the link without clicking. If the real address shown at the bottom does not match the text, it is fake.
4. Look for these signs: urgency ("click now"), generic greeting ("Dear customer"), spelling mistakes and unexpected attachments.
5. Forward the email to the security team, then delete it and empty the deleted items folder.

## Verification
The email was reported and deleted, and the user did not click anything.

## Prevention
Train users to stop and check before clicking, and turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all accounts.

## Escalation
If the user clicked the link or entered a password, follow the article "Clicked a phishing link" (KB0005) and inform the security team immediately.
